What it does

🔍

Scrapes Transactions

Opens a real browser, logs into Oracle, and pulls your corporate card transactions automatically.

🧾

Matches Receipts

Uses AI to read receipt photos and match them to the right transaction by amount, date, and vendor.

📝

Classifies Expenses

Automatically assigns the correct expense type (meals, transport, lodging, etc.) to each line.

🚀

Submits Reports

Fills in every field and submits the expense report — you just review and approve.

How it works

1

Export receipts

Take photos of your receipts or export them from your photo library. The app reads amounts, dates, and vendor names using AI.

2

Scan & match

The app logs into Oracle, scrapes your credit card transactions, and matches each one to the right receipt.

3

Review & submit

Check the matches, adjust anything that looks off, then hit submit. The app fills every form field and attaches the receipts for you.

Privacy & security

100% local

Runs entirely on your machine. No data is sent to any server other than Oracle itself and, optionally, the OpenAI API for receipt reading.

Keychain storage

Passwords are stored in your OS keychain (macOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager) — never in plain text.

Open source

Every line of code is public. Audit it yourself or have someone you trust review it.
