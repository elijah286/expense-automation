macOS Setup

Your download has started. Follow these steps to get the app running.

1 Open the DMG file
Go to your Downloads folder and double-click Expense.Automator.{{ app_version }}.dmg.
2 Handle the security warning
macOS will show this dialog:
🛡️ ⚠️
"Expense Automator"
Not Opened
Apple could not verify "Expense Automator" is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
This is normal. The app is not signed by Apple, but it's safe to run. Choose one:
Option A: Right-click Open (easier)
1. Right-click the app in Finder
2. Select Open (not double-click)
3. Click Open in the dialog
4. The app will launch normally
Option B: Terminal command (removes the warning permanently)
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Applications/"Expense Automator.app"
Then double-click to open normally.
3 Verify installation
The Expense Automator window should open. A setup dialog will ask for your credentials.
✓ You're all set! Enter your OpenAI key and Oracle portal URL to get started.

Why this happens: Apps from the internet (not the Mac App Store) need to be trusted by the system. This is a standard macOS security feature. Since this app is open-source and self-hosted, it doesn't have Apple's signature.
